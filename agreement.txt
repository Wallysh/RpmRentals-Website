 supplied with it.
Serious Breach: a breach expressly described as serious in this Agreement, or any deliberate or material breach
  creating significant safety, legal, financial or property risk.
Security Bond: the amount recorded in the Rental Schedule and provided as security for amounts properly payable
  under this Agreement.

In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

1. Parties and Agreement
This Motor Vehicle Rental Agreement (Agreement) is between RPM Rental Group Pty Ltd (Rental Provider) and the
person identified as the Renter in the Rental Schedule. The Rental Schedule, these terms, the Vehicle Condition
Report, any signed authorised-driver details and any written pre-hire approvals form one agreement.
The Renter confirms that they have had a reasonable opportunity to read this Agreement, ask questions and inspect
the Vehicle's visible condition before taking possession.
The Vehicle may be owned or registered by the Rental Provider or by another person or entity that has authorised
the Rental Provider to arrange the hire. The relevant owner or authorising entity may be identified in the Rental
Schedule. RPM Rental Group Pty Ltd remains the contracting Rental Provider under this Agreement unless the Rental
Schedule expressly states otherwise.

2. Eligibility and Authorised Drivers
2.1 The Renter must be at least 18 years old and hold a current Australian driver licence that is valid for the Vehicle
   and not suspended, cancelled or subject to a restriction that prevents the hire.
2.2 Red and green probationary licence holders may hire a Vehicle, but the Renter must not drive the Vehicle on any
   road or road-related area where doing so would breach a restriction, condition or prohibition applying to the
   Renter's licence. Learner drivers and international/overseas licences are not accepted.
2.3 Only the Renter and any additional driver expressly approved by the Rental Provider before handover may drive
   the Vehicle.
2.4 The Renter must immediately notify the Rental Provider if any licence becomes suspended, cancelled, restricted or
   otherwise invalid during the Rental Period.
2.5 Before handover, the Rental Provider may inspect, photograph and retain a copy or image of the Renter's and each
   Authorised Driver's driver licence and, where reasonably required, another form of identification, for identity
   verification, insurer requirements, incident management, debt recovery, toll/infringement nomination and
   enforcement of this Agreement. Such information must be handled in accordance with clause 21 and applicable
   law.
2.6 Where the Vehicle is, or may be, a probationary-prohibited vehicle or otherwise restricted for the Renter's licence
   class, the Renter must not drive it on a public road or road-related area unless the Renter holds a valid exemption
   or other lawful authority permitting that use.
2.7 The Renter warrants that, before driving the Vehicle on a public road or road-related area, the Renter has checked
   and satisfied themselves that their licence authorises them to drive that specific Vehicle. The Renter must disclose
   any relevant licence restriction, condition, exemption or special authority before handover.
2.8 If the Renter relies on a probationary-vehicle exemption or other special authority, the Rental Provider may
   require satisfactory evidence before permitting road use and may photograph or retain a copy of that evidence
   together with the licence copy. The Rental Provider may refuse or limit road use if satisfactory evidence is not
   provided.
2.9 The Renter is responsible for any infringement, demerit-point consequence, licence sanction, penalty or other
   consequence arising from the Renter's own unlawful use of the Vehicle or failure to comply with a restriction or
   condition applying to their licence, except to the extent caused by an unlawful act, misleading representation or
   other legally non-excludable conduct of the Rental Provider.
2.10 Nothing in this Agreement authorises or encourages unlawful driving. Permission to hire or take possession of a
   Vehicle is not a representation that the Vehicle may lawfully be driven by every licence class in every location or
   circumstance.

3. Rental Price, Deposit, Bond and Payment
3.1 The rental price, booking deposit, Security Bond, included kilometres and any other variable charges are those
   completed in the Rental Schedule.
3.2 The rental price must be paid in full before the Vehicle is handed over. Bank transfer may be accepted, but the
   Rental Provider may require cleared funds before release of the Vehicle.
3.3 The Security Bond is security for amounts properly payable under this Agreement. It is not an agreed penalty and
   is not automatically forfeited merely because a breach occurs.
3.4 Subject to any substantiated deduction or a reasonable need to retain all or part of the Security Bond while
   a damage, insurer, toll, infringement or other claim is being assessed, the Rental Provider will initiate
   return or release of the undisputed balance within 5 Business Days after the Vehicle is returned and
   inspected. Where a card pre-authorisation is used, the Rental Provider's obligation is to instruct release
   within that period; the Renter acknowledges that the card issuer or bank may take additional time to make
   the funds available.
3.5 Returning the Security Bond does not extinguish a liability that arises or becomes known later, including tolls,
   infringements, third-party charges, hidden damage reasonably attributable to the Rental Period, insurance
   excesses or recovery costs.
3.6 The Security Bond must be provided by the method stated in the Rental Schedule. Where a card pre-authorisation
   is used, the Renter authorises the Rental Provider or payment provider to place and maintain a temporary hold up
   to the stated Bond amount and, to the extent lawfully permitted and properly substantiated under this Agreement,
   to capture amounts that become payable. A pre-authorisation is not itself a charge or automatic forfeiture.

4. Cancellation and No-Show
4.1 If the Renter cancels at least 12 hours before the agreed pickup time, the booking deposit will be refunded in full.
4.2 If the Renter cancels less than 12 hours before the agreed pickup time, the Rental Provider may retain 50% of the
   booking deposit.
4.3 If the Renter does not attend for pickup and does not contact the Rental Provider within 60 minutes after the
   agreed pickup time to arrange an extension, the booking may be treated as a no-show and the booking deposit may
   be retained in full, subject to applicable law.
4.4 The Rental Provider may cancel the booking before handover if the Vehicle is unavailable because of a mechanical
   or safety issue, accident, theft, late return by a previous renter, double-booking or fleet allocation error, insurer
   restriction, legal restriction, or another circumstance that reasonably prevents the hire from proceeding.
4.5 If the Rental Provider cancels before handover, all rental amounts and booking deposits paid for the cancelled
   booking will be refunded. The Rental Provider may offer a substitute vehicle, but the Renter is not required to
   accept it.
4.6 Except for any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, the Rental Provider is not liable for indirect or
   consequential loss arising solely from a cancellation under clause 4.4 where the Rental Provider acts reasonably
   and refunds the amounts required by clause 4.5.

5. Rental Period, Return and Late Charges
5.1 The Renter must return the Vehicle to the agreed location by the return date and time in the Rental Schedule
   unless an extension is approved in writing before the due time.
5.2 A grace period of 20 minutes applies after the agreed return time. After that grace period, late return
   charges are calculated by reference to the daily rental rate: more than 20 minutes and up to 5 hours late =
   50% of one daily rate; more than 5 and up to 12 hours late = 100% of one daily rate; more than 12 and up to 17
   hours late = 150% of one daily rate; more than 17 and up to 24 hours late = 200% of one daily rate.
5.3 Late-use charges under clause 5.2 are capped at 300% of one daily rental rate for one continuous overdue
   period. The cap does not prevent early termination or lawful recovery of the Vehicle, nor recovery of
   separate, reasonably substantiated loss or recovery costs caused by the overdue possession, provided there
   is no double recovery.
5.4 Personal belongings left in the Vehicle remain at the Renter's risk. The Rental Provider will take reasonable care of
property found and, where contact details are available, make a reasonable attempt to notify the Renter. Property
may be held for up to 14 days and then disposed of, donated or otherwise dealt with as permitted by law if unclaimed.

6. Geographic and Permitted Use
6.1 The Vehicle must remain within Victoria unless the Rental Provider gives prior written permission for interstate
   travel.
6.2 The Vehicle must not be used for rideshare, taxi, food or parcel delivery, courier work, commercial carriage, sub-
   hire, rental to another person, driver training, motorsport, racing, pace-making, track days, timed events or any
   unlawful purpose.
6.3 The Vehicle must not tow any trailer, jet ski or other load unless the Rental Provider has expressly
   approved towing before handover. Towing may be approved where a jet ski is hired from the Rental
   Provider, but the approved purpose and any conditions must be recorded in the Rental Schedule or
   otherwise confirmed in writing. Any jet ski, trailer or marine equipment hired in connection with the
   booking is subject to a separate hire agreement or addendum; this Agreement governs the motor Vehicle
   only.
6.4 The Vehicle must not be driven off-road, on closed roads, through floodwater, on beaches, on unsealed terrain
   unsuitable for the Vehicle, or in any location where use is likely to cause abnormal damage.

7. Prohibited Conduct
7.1 The Renter and every Authorised Driver must obey all road laws and drive with reasonable care.
7.2 The following are prohibited: racing; drifting; burnouts; donuts; deliberate wheelspin; launch-control use;
   deliberate or sustained loss of traction; hard acceleration for the purpose of breaking traction; disabling or
   materially reducing traction control or electronic stability control; dangerous overtaking; road-rage conduct;
   intentional abuse of the engine, gearbox, clutch, tyres, brakes or driveline; and any other deliberate misuse.
7.3 Traction control and electronic stability control must remain enabled at all times while driving unless the Vehicle
   manufacturer automatically changes a setting during ordinary operation and the driver has not intentionally
   disabled it.
7.4 The Vehicle must not be driven by a person affected by alcohol, illegal drugs, impairing medication, fatigue to an
   unsafe degree, or any condition that makes driving unlawful or unsafe.
7.5 No smoking or vaping of any substance is permitted in or immediately beside the Vehicle where smoke or vapour
   may enter it. No pets or animals are permitted in the Vehicle.
7.6 No modification, coding, tuning, mechanical adjustment, tyre-pressure alteration outside manufacturer guidance,
   removal of parts, stickers, badges or plates, or tampering with vehicle systems is permitted without written
   approval.

8. Speeding, Reckless Driving and Escalation Procedure
8.1 For this clause, a 'Verified Serious Driving Event' means conduct reasonably supported by GPS/telematics, dashcam
   footage, vehicle data, police information, eyewitness evidence or a combination of those sources, and includes any
   prohibited conduct in clause 7 or serious/repeated speeding.
8.2 Serious/repeated speeding includes, after allowing for reasonable device tolerance: (a) travelling 15 km/h or more
   above the posted speed limit for 10 continuous seconds or more; (b) travelling 25 km/h or more above the posted
   speed limit at any time; or (c) three or more separate instances of travelling 10 km/h or more above the posted limit
   within 15 minutes after a warning has been given.
8.3 First Verified Serious Driving Event: the Rental Provider may issue a formal warning by telephone, SMS, messaging
   service or other reasonable method and direct the Renter to immediately stop the conduct.
8.4 Second Verified Serious Driving Event during the same Rental Period: this is a Serious Breach. The Rental Provider
   may place the amount shown in the Rental Schedule as the 'Second Driving Event security hold' on hold from the
   Security Bond pending inspection and assessment. Only amounts reasonably attributable to loss, damage,
   abnormal wear, mechanical/tyre inspection, recovery, insurer charges or other substantiated consequences of the
   conduct may ultimately be retained.
8.5 Third Verified Serious Driving Event during the same Rental Period: the Rental Provider may immediately
   terminate the hire, direct the Renter to park the Vehicle safely, and lawfully recover the Vehicle without waiting
   for the original return time.

8.6 The Rental Provider is not required to wait for a second or third event where the conduct creates an immediate
   and material safety risk, involves racing, a burnout, drifting, intentional loss of control, police pursuit/evasion,
   drink/drug driving, deliberate disabling of safety systems while driving aggressively, or other conduct that
   reasonably justifies immediate termination to protect persons or property.
8.7 Any remote immobiliser or kill-switch will only be intentionally activated when the Vehicle is stationary and when
   activation is not reasonably expected to create an immediate road-safety hazard. The Renter must not interfere
   with recovery after lawful termination.

9. Tracking, Dashcams and Security Systems
9.1 The Vehicle may contain GPS tracking, telematics, dashcams, diagnostic logging and a remote immobiliser or kill-
   switch.
9.2 These systems may be used for theft prevention, location/recovery, safety, investigating accidents or suspected
   breaches, verifying kilometres, and protecting the Vehicle and the parties' legal rights.
9.3 Where dashcam audio is enabled, that fact must be disclosed in the Rental Schedule. Audio recording will only be
   used to the extent permitted by law.
9.4 The Renter must not cover, disconnect, remove, damage, interfere with or attempt to defeat any tracker, dashcam,
   telematics device or immobiliser. Tampering is a Serious Breach.

10. Unauthorised Drivers
10.1 Allowing any person who is not an Authorised Driver to drive or take control of the Vehicle is a Serious Breach.
10.2 If an unauthorised driver is detected during the Rental Period, the Rental Provider may immediately terminate
   the hire and lawfully recover the Vehicle.
10.3 The full Security Bond may be withheld temporarily pending inspection, insurer enquiries and assessment of
   loss. It is not automatically forfeited. The Rental Provider may apply the Bond to amounts properly payable under
   this Agreement, including damage, insurance excess, recovery, towing, storage, loss of use reasonably caused by
   the breach, and other substantiated loss to the extent permitted by law.
10.4 The Renter remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any person to whom the Renter gives possession,
   access or control of the Vehicle, to the extent permitted by law.

11. Fuel, Kilometres and Consumables
11.1 Unless the Rental Schedule records otherwise, the Vehicle is supplied with a full tank and must be returned with
   a full tank of the correct fuel.
11.2 If the Vehicle is not returned full, the Rental Provider may refuel it and deduct the reasonable actual fuel cost
   from the Security Bond or invoice the Renter after return.
11.3 The Renter must use the manufacturer's required fuel grade. Any loss caused by wrong fuel or contamination is
   payable by the Renter to the extent caused by that conduct.
11.4 If the Renter exceeds the included kilometres in the Rental Schedule, the excess kilometre rate shown there
   applies.
11.5 For an electric or plug-in hybrid Vehicle, the Vehicle must be returned with at least the state of charge specified in
the Rental Schedule or, if none is specified, no less than the handover state of charge. If returned below that level, the
Renter must pay the reasonable actual charging cost plus any recharge service fee expressly stated in the Rental
Schedule.
11.6 Any charging cable, portable charger, adapter or related accessory supplied with an electric Vehicle must be
returned in the same condition, fair wear and tear excepted. Loss or damage may be charged at the reasonable
replacement or repair cost.

   cigarette butts, ash, smoke residue or odour, or other objectively excessive contamination caused during the
   Rental Period, the Renter is liable for a cleaning/remediation charge of up to $500 based on the actual or

13.3 The Renter is responsible, to the extent caused during the Rental Period by the Renter, an Authorised Driver, an

13.4 Lost or damaged keys/remotes may be charged at the reasonable replacement, programming and associated
   recovery cost.

14. Accidents, Damage, Theft and Incident Procedure
14.1 After any accident, impact, theft, attempted theft, vandalism, significant warning light, tyre failure or other
   material incident, the Renter must stop safely, protect persons and property, call emergency services where
   required, and notify the Rental Provider as soon as reasonably practicable.
14.2 The Renter must obtain the other parties' names, addresses, phone numbers, registration numbers and insurer
   details where reasonably possible; take photographs/video; identify witnesses; and provide a truthful written
   account if requested.
14.3 The Renter must not admit liability, promise payment, settle a claim or authorise repairs on the Rental Provider's
   behalf without permission, except for urgent steps reasonably necessary to protect safety.
14.4 The Renter must cooperate reasonably with the Rental Provider and any insurer, repairer, assessor, police or
   recovery provider dealing with the incident.

15. Insurance and Excess
15.1 This Agreement does not itself provide insurance. Any insurance applicable to the Vehicle is subject to the terms,
   exclusions, driver requirements and excesses of the relevant policy.
15.2 Where an insured event occurs and the Renter or an Authorised Driver is responsible for, causes or contributes to
   the event, the Renter must pay the applicable insurance excess shown in the Rental Schedule, together with any
   additional age, provisional-licence, inexperienced-driver or other excess that applies because of the driver or
   circumstances, to the extent permitted by law. Any young/inexperienced-driver threshold or special criterion
   relevant to that booking should be recorded in the Rental Schedule.
15.3 If an insurer lawfully refuses or reduces cover because of a breach by the Renter or a driver for whom the Renter
   is responsible - including an unauthorised driver, driving contrary to licence conditions or probationary-vehicle
   restrictions, drink/drug driving, racing, deliberate misuse or material non-disclosure - the Renter is liable for the
   resulting uninsured loss to the extent that the refusal or reduction was caused by or reasonably connected with
   that conduct, subject to applicable law.
15.4 Nothing in this Agreement excludes any consumer guarantee, statutory right or liability that cannot lawfully be
   excluded.
15.5 If the Vehicle is declared a total loss or write-off, or is damaged so seriously that it cannot reasonably continue in
   service, the hire terminates when the Vehicle ceases to be available for use. The Security Bond may be applied
   toward amounts properly payable under this Agreement, including an applicable insurance excess and other
   substantiated loss.
15.6 The Renter is not automatically liable for the Vehicle's full market value or for an insurer's settlement shortfall
   merely because a total loss occurs. However, to the extent permitted by law, the Renter is liable for an uninsured
   shortfall where and to the extent that the shortfall was caused by the Renter's breach, negligence, unlawful
   conduct, deliberate misuse, an unauthorised driver permitted by the Renter, or conduct that caused the insurer
   lawfully to reduce or refuse cover.

16. Fines, Tolls, Impoundment and Government Charges
16.1 The Renter is responsible for tolls, parking charges, traffic infringements, speeding infringements, camera
   offences, probationary-vehicle or licence-condition offences, and other government or road-user charges
   attributable to the Rental Period or the Renter's use of the Vehicle, except to the extent liability cannot lawfully be
   passed on.
16.2 The Renter must tell the Rental Provider as soon as reasonably practicable if they know they incurred a toll or
   infringement.
16.3 If a toll, fine or charge is received after the Vehicle has been returned or after the Security Bond has been
   refunded, the Renter remains liable and must reimburse the Rental Provider within 7 days after receiving
   reasonable evidence of the amount.
16.4 The Renter is also liable for reasonable actual administration, nomination, towing, storage, release or recovery
   costs incurred because of an infringement, impoundment or seizure caused by the Renter or a driver for whom the
   Renter is responsible.

17. Breakdowns and Mechanical Issues
17.1 The Renter must stop safely and contact the Rental Provider if a warning light, abnormal noise, overheating,
   puncture, mechanical fault or other issue may make continued driving unsafe or cause damage.
17.2 No repair may be authorised or carried out at the Rental Provider's expense without prior approval, except
   where an emergency repair is reasonably necessary to protect safety and prior approval cannot reasonably be
   obtained.
17.3 The Rental Provider is responsible for ordinary mechanical failure not caused or contributed to by the Renter,
   subject to applicable law. The Renter is responsible for damage or cost caused by misuse, wrong fuel, ignoring
   warnings, continuing to drive when unsafe, impact damage or other breach.
17.4 For roadside assistance, the Renter must first contact the Rental Provider using the Provider contact details in the
Rental Schedule unless an emergency requires immediate action. If a roadside assistance provider or number is listed
in the Rental Schedule, the Renter may also contact that provider as directed. The Renter must not arrange non-
emergency towing or repair at the Rental Provider's expense without approval.

18. Termination and Recovery Rights
18.1 The Rental Provider may terminate the Rental Period early where the Renter commits a Serious Breach, fails to
   return the Vehicle, leaves Victoria without approval, permits an unauthorised driver, tampers with
   tracking/security systems, uses the Vehicle unlawfully, fails to pay an amount due before handover, or engages in
   conduct creating a material risk to persons or property.
18.2 On termination, the Renter must immediately cease use, park the Vehicle safely as directed, secure it, provide its
   location and keys, and allow lawful recovery.
18.3 The Renter must reimburse reasonable towing, recovery, transport and storage costs caused by the Renter's
   Serious Breach, to the extent permitted by law.
18.4 The Rental Provider will exercise recovery rights lawfully and will not intentionally create a road-safety hazard.

19. Security Bond Deductions and Evidence
19.1 The Rental Provider may apply the Security Bond only toward amounts properly payable under this Agreement,
   including unpaid rent/late charges, excess kilometres, fuel, substantiated cleaning/remediation, damage, insurance
   excess, lost keys/accessories, tolls/fines, and reasonable recovery costs.
19.2 Where a deduction is not a fixed amount already known and agreed in the Rental Schedule, the Rental Provider
   will provide an itemised explanation and reasonable supporting evidence such as photographs, video, a quote,
   invoice, assessment, infringement notice or toll statement.
19.3 The Renter may dispute a proposed or completed deduction by giving written reasons and any supporting
   evidence. The parties must deal with the dispute in good faith.

20. Liability, Indemnity and Consumer Law
20.1 The Renter is liable for loss or damage that is caused by the Renter's breach, negligence, unlawful conduct or
   misuse, or by a person for whom the Renter is responsible, subject to this Agreement, applicable insurance and
   law.
20.2 To the extent permitted by law, the Renter indemnifies the Rental Provider against third-party claims, loss and
   reasonable expense caused by the Renter's unlawful use, breach, negligence or deliberate misuse of the Vehicle.
   This indemnity does not apply to loss caused by the Rental Provider's negligence, breach of law, pre-existing defect,
   or a matter for which liability cannot lawfully be excluded.
20.3 Nothing in this Agreement excludes, restricts or modifies rights or remedies under the Australian Consumer Law
   or any other law where that exclusion, restriction or modification would be unlawful.

21. Privacy and Personal Information
21.1 The Rental Provider may collect and use identification, licence, contact, payment, booking, vehicle-location,
   telematics, incident and condition information for administering the hire, protecting and recovering the Vehicle,
   processing claims, complying with law, resolving disputes and enforcing this Agreement.
21.2 Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to insurers, police, road/toll authorities,
   courts/tribunals, lawyers, recovery providers, repairers, payment providers and other service providers involved
   in the hire or a claim, subject to applicable privacy and surveillance laws.
21.3 Licence and identification copies may be retained for so long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this
   Agreement, insurer or legal requirements, or an unresolved claim, toll, infringement, debt or dispute, and should
   then be securely destroyed or de-identified when no longer reasonably required, subject to applicable law.

22. Notices
22.1 A notice or approval under this Agreement may be given in writing by SMS, email, messaging application
   or signed document to the contact details provided in the Rental Schedule or subsequently notified in
   writing by either party. The Rental Provider's notice details are the Provider mobile and Provider email
   recorded in the Rental Schedule.
22.2 Urgent safety or recovery directions may also be given by telephone and confirmed in writing as soon as
   reasonably practicable.

23. Disputes
23.1 A party who disputes a charge, deduction or alleged breach must give the other party reasonable details of the
   dispute and any supporting evidence.
23.2 The parties must first attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute directly. This clause does not prevent either
   party from seeking urgent relief or exercising any right to approach Consumer Affairs Victoria, VCAT, a court or
   another competent authority.

24. Force Majeure
24.1 Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform an obligation, other than an obligation to pay an amount
already due, to the extent caused by an event beyond that party's reasonable control, including severe weather, flood,
fire, natural disaster, road closure, government restriction, civil emergency, widespread infrastructure failure, or
other comparable event that could not reasonably have been prevented or overcome.
24.2 A party affected by such an event must take reasonable steps to reduce its impact and notify the other party as
soon as reasonably practicable. This clause does not excuse payment obligations that accrued before the event or
liability for a party's own negligence, breach or unlawful conduct.
24.3 If a force majeure event prevents handover before the Rental Period begins, the Rental Provider will
refund amounts paid for the affected booking in accordance with clause 4. If it materially interrupts a hire
already in progress, the parties will act reasonably to arrange return, recovery or a substitute vehicle if
available.
