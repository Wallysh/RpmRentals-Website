JET SKI HIRE AGREEMENT
Rental Provider: RPM Rental Group Pty Ltd. Jurisdiction: Victoria, Australia.
The booking details shown on this page form the Rental Schedule. "Jet Ski" means the jet ski described in the Rental Schedule, and "Hirer" means the person signing this Agreement.

2. ELIGIBILITY AND LICENCE REQUIREMENTS

2.1 The Hirer must be at least 18 years old and provide valid identification.

2.2 The Hirer must hold a current marine licence with a Personal Watercraft (PWC) endorsement recognised in Victoria.

2.3 The Hirer must provide their licence for inspection before the Jet Ski is released.

2.4 Only the approved Hirer may operate the Jet Ski unless another operator has been approved in writing by the Rental Provider.

2.5 The Hirer must not allow an unlicensed or unauthorised person to operate the Jet Ski.

2.6 The Rental Provider may refuse handover if the Hirer does not meet the requirements or appears unfit to operate the Jet Ski safely.

3. PAYMENT, DEPOSIT AND SECURITY BOND

3.1 The full rental amount must be paid before the Jet Ski is handed over.

3.2 A booking deposit is required to secure the reservation.

3.3 The Security Bond is held as security against damage, loss, unpaid charges or other amounts properly payable under this Agreement.

3.4 The Security Bond is not automatically forfeited because of a breach.

3.5 The Rental Provider may deduct properly substantiated amounts for:
- Damage to the Jet Ski.
- Missing or damaged equipment.
- Unpaid rental charges.
- Cleaning costs.
- Recovery or towing costs.
- Other reasonable costs caused by the Hirer's breach.

3.6 The undisputed bond balance will be released within 5 business days after return and inspection, subject to any reasonable investigation of outstanding claims.

3.7 Any later-discovered damage or charges attributable to the rental period remain the Hirer's responsibility, subject to applicable law.

4. CANCELLATIONS

4.1 Cancellations made at least 48 hours before pickup will receive a full booking deposit refund.

4.2 Cancellations made less than 48 hours before pickup may result in the booking deposit being retained to cover reasonable cancellation losses.

4.3 Failure to attend without notice may be treated as a no-show.

4.4 If the Rental Provider cancels due to mechanical failure, unsafe conditions or circumstances preventing the hire, all amounts paid for the affected booking will be refunded.

5. PICKUP AND RETURN

5.1 The Jet Ski must be collected and returned at the agreed location and time.

5.2 Any extension must be approved by the Rental Provider in writing before the agreed return time.

5.3 The Hirer must return the Jet Ski in the same condition as received, allowing for fair wear and tear.

5.4 The Jet Ski must be returned with the agreed fuel level.

5.5 Late returns may incur the late return charge recorded in the Rental Schedule.

5.6 The Hirer must not abandon the Jet Ski or leave it unsecured.

6. SAFETY AND OPERATING RULES

The Hirer agrees to:

6.1 Wear an approved lifejacket at all times while operating or riding on the Jet Ski.

6.2 Attach and use the engine safety lanyard correctly.

6.3 Follow all Victorian marine laws, speed limits, distance rules and local waterway restrictions.

6.4 Operate the Jet Ski responsibly and maintain a safe distance from swimmers, other vessels, shorelines and restricted areas.

6.5 Never operate the Jet Ski under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs or impairing medication.

6.6 Never operate the Jet Ski in a dangerous, reckless or careless manner.

6.7 Not race, perform dangerous manoeuvres, deliberately jump wakes at close range or intentionally put passengers or other water users at risk.

6.8 Not overload the Jet Ski or exceed its manufacturer's passenger or weight capacity.

6.9 Not remove, damage or interfere with safety equipment, GPS trackers or security devices.

6.10 Follow all instructions given during the pre-hire safety briefing.

7. PROHIBITED USE

The Hirer must not:
- Allow an unauthorised person to operate the Jet Ski.
- Use the Jet Ski for racing or competitive events.
- Operate the Jet Ski outside approved waterways or restricted areas.
- Use the Jet Ski for commercial purposes or subhire it.
- Intentionally beach, ground or strike the Jet Ski.
- Make modifications or attempt repairs without approval.
- Tow another vessel or person without prior written approval and compliance with applicable safety requirements.
- Take the Jet Ski into unsafe weather or water conditions.

Any serious breach may result in immediate termination of the hire and recovery of the Jet Ski.

8. DAMAGE, LOSS AND THEFT

8.1 The Hirer is responsible for damage caused during the rental period by their negligence, misuse, unlawful conduct or breach of this Agreement.

8.2 This includes damage to:
- Hull and bodywork.
- Engine and propulsion system.
- Impeller and wear ring.
- Steering and throttle systems.
- Seats and accessories.
- Trailer and supplied equipment.

8.3 The Hirer must immediately notify the Rental Provider of any damage, loss, theft or mechanical issue.

8.4 The Hirer must not authorise repairs without written approval, except for urgent action reasonably necessary to protect safety.

8.5 The Hirer must cooperate with any police report, insurance claim or investigation.

8.6 The Hirer is not responsible for pre-existing damage, fair wear and tear or mechanical failure not caused or contributed to by the Hirer.

9. ACCIDENTS AND EMERGENCIES

9.1 If an accident or emergency occurs, the Hirer must prioritise the safety of everyone involved.

9.2 The Hirer must contact emergency services where necessary.

9.3 The Hirer must notify the Rental Provider as soon as reasonably possible.

9.4 The Hirer must provide:
- Details of the incident.
- Location and time.
- Photographs or videos where possible.
- Details of any other persons or vessels involved.
- Police or incident report details, if applicable.

9.5 The Hirer must not leave the Jet Ski unattended in an unsafe location.

10. FUEL, CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE

10.1 The Jet Ski must be returned with the agreed fuel level.

10.2 Any fuel shortfall may be charged to the Hirer at the reasonable cost of refuelling.

10.3 The Jet Ski must be returned reasonably clean and free of rubbish.

10.4 Excessive dirt, damage, staining or contamination may result in reasonable cleaning or repair charges.

10.5 The Hirer must immediately report warning lights, unusual noises, overheating or mechanical problems.

10.6 The Hirer must not continue operating the Jet Ski if doing so could cause further damage or create a safety risk.

11. GPS TRACKING AND SECURITY

11.1 The Jet Ski may contain GPS tracking or other security devices.

11.2 Tracking may be used for theft prevention, location monitoring, recovery, safety and investigating suspected breaches.

11.3 The Hirer must not remove, disconnect, cover or interfere with any tracking or security equipment.

11.4 Tampering with these devices is considered a serious breach of this Agreement.

12. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

12.1 Any insurance applicable to the Jet Ski is subject to the relevant policy terms, exclusions and conditions.

12.2 This Agreement does not create insurance cover or guarantee that every accident or loss is insured.

12.3 The Hirer may be responsible for the applicable insurance excess where their conduct or the circumstances of the incident make them liable under this Agreement and the relevant policy.

12.4 The Hirer is responsible for loss caused by their negligence, misuse, unlawful conduct or breach of this Agreement, subject to applicable law.

12.5 Nothing in this Agreement excludes rights under the Australian Consumer Law or any liability that cannot legally be excluded.

13. TERMINATION

13.1 The Rental Provider may immediately terminate the hire if the Hirer:
- Provides false information.
- Allows an unauthorised operator.
- Operates the Jet Ski dangerously.
- Breaches important safety requirements.
- Fails to return the Jet Ski.
- Interferes with tracking or security equipment.
- Uses the Jet Ski unlawfully.

13.2 Upon termination, the Hirer must stop operating the Jet Ski, secure it safely and follow the Rental Provider's lawful recovery instructions.

13.3 The Hirer may be responsible for reasonable recovery and transport costs caused by their breach.

14. PRIVACY

14.1 The Rental Provider may collect identification, licence, contact, payment, booking, GPS and incident information for managing the hire.

14.2 Information may be shared with insurers, police, authorities, lawyers and recovery providers where reasonably necessary and legally permitted.

14.3 Personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

15. DISPUTES AND GOVERNING LAW

15.1 Both parties agree to make reasonable efforts to resolve disputes directly.

15.2 Either party may seek assistance from Consumer Affairs Victoria, VCAT or another relevant authority.

15.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
